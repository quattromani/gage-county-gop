# Filled recommendation ovals

Max explicitly confirmed on October 26, 2026 that the candidates on the Republican card are the recommended selections and requested filled ovals online and in print. The publication configuration records the 83 confirmed candidacy IDs, separately from the neutral candidate source. Builders fail if that set changes so new candidates are not automatically represented as recommendations.

Use solid ink ovals in the web card, browser print, letter flyer, mobile PDF, and long PNG. A text legend identifies their meaning without relying on color. These marks are noninteractive. Preserve ballot voting limits, districts, write-in instructions, and affiliation evidence. Judicial retention remains informational with no marked Yes or No choice.

Validation: npm run check (15 tests); scripts/test-gop-artifacts.py verifies 83 listings, 82 people, six exact retention questions, two flyer pages and matching downloads. Both flyer pages and the mobile opening were rendered and visually reviewed.
